## Slide 4 — Our two extensions and objective

**Double DQN:** online network selects the next action; target network evaluates it.  
**3-step return:** propagates sparse rewards to earlier actions.

$$
a^* = \arg\max_a\; Q_{\mathrm{online}}(o_{t+h},\, a)
$$
$$
y = R_t^{(h)} + \Gamma_t\, Q_{\mathrm{target}}(o_{t+h},\, a^*)
$$
$$
L = \mathbb{E}_{\mathrm{replay}}\!\left[\operatorname{Huber}\!\left(y - Q_{\mathrm{online}}(o_t,\, a_t)\right)\right]
$$

$$
R_t^{(h)} = \sum_{k=0}^{h-1} \gamma^k r_{t+k+1}, \qquad \Gamma_t = \gamma^h m_t
$$

*Caption:* h ≤ 3: actual return horizon. mₜ = 0 at death, 1 otherwise.

*Visual:* [training_objective.png](../figures/training_objective.png).

*Say:* `h` is the actual horizon in `NStepAccumulator` (`h = 3`, or `1`/`2` on the last two transitions of an episode), so both `o_{t+h}`, `R_t^{(h)}` and `Γ_t` always stay consistent. `m_t = 0` cuts bootstrapping at death, while `m_t = 1` keeps bootstrapping on the 10,000-step time-limit truncation. We did not isolate either extension in an equal-budget ablation.

## Slide 5 — Experimental protocol

**Training:** 2 independent seeds × 1,000,000 steps.  
**Model selection:** 30 validation episodes (seeds 3001–3030), checkpoints at 300k, 500k and 1M.  
**Final test:** 50 fresh episodes (seeds 7001–7050) per trained seed, greedy policy (ε = 0).  
**Random reference:** the same 50 final episode seeds.

*Say:* The final episodes were not used to select the checkpoint.

## Slide 10 — Behavior, limits, conclusion

**Observed sequence:** sapling → plant; wood → table → wood pickaxe in one traced episode.  
**Failure mode:** repeated drink-collection action in that same episode.  
**Conclusion:** 4.14% Crafter score vs 1.23% random on fresh episodes.  
**Next study:** an equal-budget DQN ablation or an observation history.

*Say:* The sequence does not prove deliberate planning. The single-image network has no explicit memory. Other observed behaviours, from the 100 final episodes and the three traced episodes (seeds 8001–8003): the agent defeats a zombie in 44% of episodes and eats a cow in 23% (random: 0% for both); it plants saplings far more than random (84% vs 50%), sometimes in repeated sapling → plant cycles (five times in traced episode 2); it crafts a second wood pickaxe at step 223 of traced episode 1 although only the first unlock is rewarded; it wakes up less often than random (77% vs 94%) because the greedy policy picks `sleep` in only 1.7% of traced steps against 1/17 ≈ 5.9% for random; about a third of the traced steps are crafting or placing attempts that cannot succeed with the current inventory, so they act as no-ops. It survives slightly longer than random (191 vs 170 steps). The assignment's “coding agent” baseline has no published numeric result in the supplied material. A peer's reported 6.1% at 1M steps uses an unknown evaluation protocol; our result is below it and is not directly comparable.
